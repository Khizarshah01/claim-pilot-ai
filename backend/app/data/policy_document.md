# ClaimPilot Standard Personal Auto Policy — Form PA-2026

## Part A — Physical Damage Coverage

### A.1 Insuring Agreement — Collision
We will pay for direct and accidental loss to your covered auto caused by collision with another vehicle or object, including a rear-end collision, side-swipe, impact with a pole, guardrail, wall or barrier, or upset of the vehicle. Collision coverage applies only if the declarations page shows a collision premium and limit for the covered auto.

### A.2 Insuring Agreement — Comprehensive (Other Than Collision)
We will pay for direct and accidental loss to your covered auto not caused by collision. Loss caused by theft, fire, flood, hail, windstorm, falling objects such as a tree branch, vandalism, glass breakage, a windshield struck by a rock, or contact with an animal such as a deer is covered under comprehensive and is not considered collision.

### A.3 Deductible
Our payment for each loss will be reduced by the deductible shown on the declarations page for the applicable coverage. The deductible applies separately to each loss and is subtracted from the approved repair cost before any payment is made.

### A.4 Limit of Liability
Our limit of liability for loss is the lesser of the actual cash value of the damaged property, the amount necessary to repair or replace the property with like kind and quality, or the limit shown on the declarations page. In no event will we pay more than the coverage limit, after the deductible is applied.

### A.5 Payment of Loss
We may pay for loss in money or repair or replace the damaged property. Payment will be made to you and any loss payee shown on the declarations page within 30 days after an agreed settlement.

## Part B — Exclusions

### B.1 Exclusion — Wear and Tear, Mechanical Breakdown
We will not pay for damage due and confined to wear and tear, freezing, mechanical or electrical breakdown or failure, engine failure, transmission failure, rust, or corrosion, unless the damage results from a covered theft or collision.

### B.2 Exclusion — Racing and Speed Contests
We will not pay for loss to your covered auto while it is used in any organized or prearranged racing, speed contest, timed event, track day, drag race, or practice for such an event, whether on a public road, racetrack or raceway.

### B.3 Exclusion — Commercial Use, Rideshare and Delivery
We will not pay for loss to your covered auto while it is being used to carry persons or property for a fee, including while logged into a rideshare or delivery network such as Uber, Lyft, DoorDash or Instacart, unless a commercial or rideshare endorsement is attached to this policy.

### B.4 Exclusion — Intoxicated Operation
We will not pay for loss sustained while the covered auto is operated by a person under the influence of alcohol, intoxicated, impaired, or charged with driving under the influence (DUI) of drugs or alcohol.

### B.5 Exclusion — Intentional Acts
We will not pay for loss caused intentionally or deliberately by you or any person acting at your direction, or for any loss arising from a staged accident or fraudulent claim.

### B.6 Exclusion — Unlicensed or Excluded Drivers
We will not pay for loss sustained while the covered auto is operated by an unlicensed driver or a driver specifically excluded by endorsement on this policy.

## Part C — Conditions

### C.1 Policy Period and Territory
This policy applies only to accidents and losses which occur during the policy period shown on the declarations page and within the United States, its territories, and Canada. The policy must be in force on the date of loss.

### C.2 Premium Payment
Coverage is conditioned on payment of the premium when due. If the premium is unpaid or past due on the date of loss, no coverage is afforded and the claim will not be paid.

### C.3 Duties After an Accident or Loss
You must give us prompt notice of the accident or loss, including how, when and where it happened. In the event of theft, or a collision involving another party with damage above $10,000, you must promptly notify the police and provide a police report number.

### C.4 Fraud and Misrepresentation
We do not provide coverage for any insured who has made fraudulent statements or engaged in fraudulent conduct in connection with any claim. Claims presenting inconsistencies, inflated repair estimates, or unverifiable descriptions may be referred to a special investigations adjuster before payment.

### C.5 Appraisal and Independent Estimate
If we and you disagree on the amount of loss, either may demand an appraisal. We may require an independent repair estimate or inspection of the damaged vehicle before approving a repair cost that exceeds typical market rates for the reported damage.
